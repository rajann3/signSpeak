    About Holistic Body & Hand Recognition

    How Body + 2-Hand Detection Works

    This app uses MediaPipe Holistic to track 33 body pose landmarks, 468 face landmarks, and 21 landmarks per hand (left & right). By combining body coordinates with finger geometry, the app knows whether your hand is touching your forehead (Hello), chin (Thank You / Home / Eat), chest (Sorry / Please / Love), or interacting with your second hand (Stop / Help / More / Friend).

    How to Separate Numbers & Letters

    In sign language, some numbers and letters share similar finger extensions (such as V and 2, or B and 4). Our geometric engine uses Palm Orientation: face your palm toward the camera for Alphabet letters, and face the back of your hand toward the camera (palm facing you) for Numbers 1–9!
